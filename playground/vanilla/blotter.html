profiler blotter

This page draws a blotter of 5,000 rows and times its work with probes. It shows the self time, an async span, a span that ends after the paint, the frame phases and the frame pacing. Each number is a measurement of this browser and this machine.

Back to the playground

Actions

Idle.

Probes

Each row is one ui.profile.duration record of this run. The durations are in milliseconds. The page shows no average. self p99 is the time in the span and not in a span inside it. An async probe and a span that ends after the paint have none.

Frame phases

Each row sums the ui.profile.frame.duration points of one probe and one phase. A row shows only for a long animation frame with a span of that probe. Firefox and Safari report no long animation frames.
